01 RuView — Setup

Three devices.
One demo.

Orange Pi runs the sensing server. ESP32-C6 streams 60 GHz radar data over MQTT. iOS Simulator on the Mac reads it over WebSocket. All three on a single phone hotspot.

Sensor
ESP32-C6 + MR60BHA260 GHz FMCW radar
Server
Orange Pi 5simha.local
Client
iOS Simulatoron the Mac running Xcode
Network
Phone hotspotportable: no router, no wires
02 RuView — What it sees

Radar-grade
presence detection.

The 60 GHz radar locks onto the chest's micro-motion from breathing. That gives us presence, distance, breathing rate, and heart rate — all from one sensor, all updated in real time.

●
Presence
Detects a person within the 120° cone. 4-second stickiness rides out micro lock-loss.
⤢
Range
0.5 – 3 m. Distance reported in centimeters, ±10 cm typical accuracy.
♥
Heart rate
40 – 200 BPM. Plausibility-gated against radar lock-on noise.
~
Breathing rate
8 – 35 BPM. Updates within a few seconds of subject becoming still.
